Welcome to Style Club!

To play, double-click index.html. Choose Microsoft Edge, Chrome, Firefox, or Safari if your computer asks which app to use.

Keep the game files together in this folder. If you received a ZIP file, extract the whole ZIP first.

No Roblox, account, installation, or internet connection is needed.

Explore your 3D boutique! Click the floor to walk or click a clothing rack to visit it. You can also click the room and use WASD, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows.

Drag to look around. Near a rack, press E or the Try button. Choose a piece in the dressing room to wear it. Outfit view lets you turn the character and see every side.

NEW: Drag clothes straight from a 3D rack onto your character! You can also drag the dressing-room cards with the mouse. Look for the "Drop to wear" outline. Near a rack, "More on this rack" shows more choices.

Try the Makeup tab and Face view for face paint and cheek colors. Fresh face washes it off. Strike a pose opens eight poses, including a twirl and a superhero pose.

Poppy, Nova, and Jules are computer-controlled shoppers who walk around and try new outfits. They are part of the game, not people on the internet. Tap "3 boutique friends" for a quiet room.

Use Full screen to fill the display. Exit full screen or Escape takes you back. If your browser cannot use full screen, the game expands to fill the window instead.

Choose a theme, try on clothes, and visit the runway. Free play gives you unlimited time. The three-minute challenge is completely optional.

The 3D game needs a browser that supports WebGL 2. If it says graphics could not start, try a recent Edge or Chrome browser.

Save your favorite outfits in My lookbook. The lookbook stays in this browser on this device. Download your favorite outfit pictures before clearing browser data or moving to a different computer.

Have fun making something that's all you!
