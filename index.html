WELCOME TO YOUR LITTLE WORLD

Let’s play dress-up.

  1. 01

    Explore your boutique

    Click the floor to walk, or use WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Drag the room to look around. Visit a rack and press E to browse. More on this rack brings out more pieces!

  2. 02

    Make a look you love

    Drag a piece from a rack or the dressing room onto your character. You can tap a card to wear it, too. Outfit view lets you see every side. Try Makeup and Face view for face paint; Fresh face washes it off.

  3. 03

    Take your runway moment

    Choose one of eight poses with Strike a pose, then visit the runway. Save your favorite outfits and poses in My lookbook.

Full screen fills the display; Escape takes you back. Free play has no time limit. The boutique friends button lets you choose company or a quiet room.

A NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

Just a little room to play.

Style Club is an original, single-player dress-up game. It has no accounts, multiplayer, chat, ads, purchases, analytics, or external content.

Poppy, Nova, and Jules are computer-controlled shoppers who wander and try clothes. They are part of the game, not people on the internet. The boutique friends button switches to a quiet room.

All clothing is available from the start. Style stars celebrate outfit choices and the theme; they never judge a character’s body or appearance. The timer is optional and pauses when you switch away.

The current outfit and up to 40 saved looks stay in this browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing browser data removes saved looks, so use the download button in the lookbook to keep favorites.

Once the files are on your computer, you can open index.html directly and play offline. This is an independent project, unaffiliated with Roblox or Dress to Impress.
