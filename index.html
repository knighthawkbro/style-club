WELCOME TO YOUR LITTLE WORLD

Let’s play dress-up.

  1. 01

    Explore your fashion mall

    Choose a shop on the Mall map, click the floor to walk, or use WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Drag the room to look around. Every piece is out on the racks and shelves. Shopping friends invites Poppy, Nova, or Jules along. Ask for an outfit idea or sit together on a bench!

  2. 02

    Make a look you love

    Drag a piece from a rack onto your character, or tap its card to wear it. Sit in the Salon chair for hair and colors. At the Makeup vanity, pick a design and color, then drag a brush onto your character or tap Apply. Try pieces and poses at a Dressing mirror. Be a stylist lets you dress Poppy, Nova, or Jules for a Halloween party, concert, or picnic. Match my outfit makes matching clothes; Done returns to your character. Visit Sole Mates for heels, Charm & Co. for jewelry and pets, or BOO-tique for costumes. Tap a worn accessory to remove it.

  3. 03

    Make a memory together

    Choose one of sixteen poses with Strike a pose, then visit the cheering runway with your shopping friend. Everyone to runway brings all three friends and saves your group together. Your pose returns when you stop walking. The Photo booth has backgrounds, friends, and stickers to drag into place. Your held pet comes too! Save outfits and photos in My lookbook.

Full screen fills the display; Escape takes you back. Free play has no time limit. Click a nearby friend or press F to say hi. Friends notice your new clothes, hair and pets. The mall friends button lets you choose company or a quiet room. Done or walking gets you up from a chair.

A NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

Just a little room to play.

Style Club is an original, single-player dress-up game. It has no accounts, multiplayer, chat, ads, purchases, analytics, or external content.

Poppy, Nova, and Jules are computer-controlled shoppers who wander, try clothes, and can come along as companions. Their friendly comments are prewritten. They are part of the game, not people on the internet. The mall friends button switches to a quiet room.

All clothing, pets, and the VIP lounge are available from the start. Style stars celebrate outfit choices and the theme; they never judge a character’s body or appearance. The timer is optional and pauses when you switch away.

The current outfit and up to 40 saved outfits and booth photos stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. In My lookbook, use Download lookbook backup to keep a copy before clearing site data. Restore a backup brings those looks back, or copies them into another browser, without replacing existing looks. Individual PNG downloads keep a finished picture. An ordinary refresh or game update keeps browser saves; clearing site data removes them.

Once the files are on your computer, you can open index.html directly and play offline. This is an independent project, unaffiliated with Roblox or Dress to Impress.
